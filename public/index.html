Facts checked
15 Oct 2026
  • One source of truth. 36 questions and answers, built from the official course page.
  • Your 5-question answer uses fixed rules, not AI, so it can only repeat checked facts.
  • Typed questions are fact-checked. AI writes a draft, and the server rejects it if any price, number or promise doesn’t match the facts. You see the checked answer instead.
  • Gaps are named, not filled. If the page doesn’t say something (like device rules or discounts), the assistant tells you and points you to the Lapaas team.
  • No personal data. It never asks for your name, phone or email.
