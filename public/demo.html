02 / 09

The problem

A student’s doubts are personal.

  • “I don’t know coding. Can I really do this?”
  • “Will this help me get a job?”
  • “Is ₹2,999 worth it for my goal?”

The course page shows the same FAQ to everyone. The only next steps are the ₹2,999 buy button or WhatsApp.

Audience I chose: students & freshers, a group the course page names directly.

03 / 09

Research & key insight

Every competitor gives a free, personal first step.

100xEngineers₹1,59,490 live cohort → free masterclass + application + personal roadmap
CodebasicsUS$50 self-paced → many free preview lectures
OutskillFree 2-day workshop → paid programme later

Lapaas goes straight from information to a ₹2,999 buy button.

Prices as shown on each official page, checked 14 Oct 2026.

04 / 09

My solution

An instant, honest answer.

5 quick taps→Honest answer→Ask anything→Next step
  • Every fact comes from the official course page (36 checked questions).
  • If the page doesn’t say it, it answers “I can’t verify that” and points to the Lapaas team.
  • Jargon like RAG or MCP is explained in simple words.
  • Collects no name, phone or email. One script tag to embed on the course page.
05 / 09

How it stays honest

A wrong price or a promised job costs more trust than it wins.

  • The 5-question answer uses fixed rules, no AI. It can only quote checked facts.
  • Typed questions: Gemini drafts an answer, then my server checks every number, price and promise against the cited facts.
  • If a check fails, the visitor sees the verified answer instead.

Ask “Is access 6 months?” → it says 3 months.
